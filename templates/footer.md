## Contributing

Entries live in [`data/entries/`](data/entries/), one YAML file per section; `README.md` and the pages under [`sections/`](sections/) are generated. The inclusion criteria are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md): open source, runs, honest about its mode, no signal-selling. Open a pull request with one entry, or an issue if you are not sure it fits.

A weekly job runs [`scripts/scout.py`](scripts/scout.py) to find new and fast-rising repositories and [`scripts/check_stale.py`](scripts/check_stale.py) to flag listed ones that went quiet; both post their report as an issue.
